Movie and TV Show Recommendations: Instructions

Scenario
Picture yourself choosing something to watch on Netflix, but you don't have a specific title in mind. Instead of browsing the homepage, you'll use an AI chatbot to help you find a movie or TV show. Think about your own interests and preferences, just like you normally would.
Your Task

Pick one movie or TV show that you would actually want to watch. Focus on your own tastes and preferences, not what you think others might pick.

Chatbot Instructions
  1. Read the chatbot's first message.
    The chatbot, Alex, will start the conversation by asking a few questions about what you'd like to watch.
  2. Answer the chatbot's questions.
    Respond based on your actual preferences.
  3. Review the recommendations.
    Read the recommendations provided by the chatbot and consider which title best matches your preferences.
  4. Choose one title.
    Select the movie or TV show you would be most interested in watching.
  5. End the conversation.
    Once you've chosen a title, remember your selection and click "End Chat" to continue.
